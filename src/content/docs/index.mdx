The guide follows the order of the 4-Flight training booklet (*4F pour les nuls*, 3.0
edition): first the position, then what the picture shows, then the tools in the order a
controller meets them. If you know a chapter of the booklet, the chapter of the same
number here shows how g-atm does it. Every picture is a capture of the application.
